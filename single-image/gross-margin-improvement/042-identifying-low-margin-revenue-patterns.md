## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

When an account executive asks for a 'quick 15% discount to get the deal signed by Friday,' most leaders think they are giving up 15%.

In financial reality, they are often giving away 50% of their operating profit.

Here is the unyielding commercial math:
- Base Contract: $100,000 ARR
- Cost of Goods Sold (Cloud hosting, third-party APIs): $20,000
- Operating Expenses (Sales commission, onboarding, customer support): $50,000
- Net Operating Profit: $30,000 (30% margin)

Now look at the 15% discounted contract:
- Discounted Contract: $85,000 ARR
- Cost of Goods Sold: $20,000 (unchanged)
- Operating Expenses: $50,000 (unchanged)
- Net Operating Profit: $15,000

A 15% top-line concession cut net operating profit by exactly 50%!

To make up for that casual discount, your sales team must close twice as many deals, handle twice as many onboarding tickets, and support twice as many customers.

Price discounting is not a closing tactic. It is a margin catastrophe.

How strictly does your deal desk govern price concessions?
