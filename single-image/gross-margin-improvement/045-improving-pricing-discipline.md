## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

A $250,000 enterprise contract looks spectacular on the sales scoreboard.

But if that customer requires $180,000 a year in dedicated customer success managers, bespoke script maintenance, and cloud compute overages, you didn't win an enterprise asset.

You won an operational liability.

Cost-to-Serve (CTS) is the blind spot of enterprise SaaS finance:

1. Dedicated Personnel Drag: Accounts that demand bi-weekly bespoke business reviews and full-time Slack channel support consume 4x the standard CSM capacity.
2. Bespoke Technical Debt: Custom connectors written to appease a single customer's legacy database require ongoing engineering maintenance every time your core platform updates.
3. Compute & Data Ingestion Inefficiencies: Unchecked API querying and unoptimized data storage that inflate your AWS/GCP bills.
4. Executive Overhead: Senior VP and C-level hours spent defusing avoidable onboarding escalations.

When CFOs audit Cost-to-Serve at the account level, they can establish minimum contract thresholds, automate high-frequency workflows, and renegotiate contracts that drain profitability.
