Per-seat pricing is actively killing B2B software expansion.

When you charge strictly per user seat:
- Your customers share passwords to avoid buying additional licenses.
- Department heads restrict user access to cut software costs.
- You actively penalize the customer for adopting your platform across their enterprise.

Modern pricing architecture aligns the commercial contract with customer value realization:

  1. Flawed Legacy Model (Per-Seat): Creates artificial friction. The customer's financial incentive is to limit platform usage, directly contradicting their success.
  2. Pure Usage Model (Unpredictable): Highly variable bills make enterprise budgeting difficult for CFOs, creating quarterly billing friction.
  3. Hybrid Platform Model (Optimal): A predictable annual platform base fee that covers core enterprise capabilities, paired with transparent tiered value metrics (e.g., pipeline volume analyzed, records enriched, transactions processed).

When your pricing expands naturally as the customer gets more business value, account expansion requires zero friction.
