Gross margin expansion is the ultimate lever for increasing enterprise valuation.

A company with 85% gross margins is valued at significantly higher multiples than a peer with 65% margins—even if their top-line ARR is identical.

Where does the margin difference come from? Delivery efficiency:

In the Legacy Model (62% Margin):
- Onboarding takes 60 to 90 days of manual coordination.
- CSMs spend 15 hours a week manually assembling quarterly review decks.
- Technical implementation requires custom engineering scripts for every account.
- The company must hire one new CSM for every 15 new accounts, resulting in linear headcount inflation.

In the AI-Native Model (86% Margin):
- Autonomous telemetry sets up integrations and validates data in 12 days.
- Executive review decks are automatically generated from verified product usage.
- One CSM effectively manages 60+ accounts with higher customer satisfaction.

When technology absorbs operational delivery overhead, gross margins expand by 1,500 to 2,500 basis points—dropping directly to your EBITDA bottom line.
