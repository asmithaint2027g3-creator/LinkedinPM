## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

In almost every enterprise B2B company, the top 20% of accounts generate 120% of total operating profit.

The bottom 20% actively destroys capital:
- They require continuous custom engineering.
- They submit 10x more customer support tickets than average accounts.
- They demanded heavy discounts during the sales cycle and will resist any price increase at renewal.

If you evaluate customers solely on ARR, you cannot see who is building enterprise value and who is consuming it.

Apply the Customer Profitability Framework:

1. Strategic Compounders: High ARR, low custom delivery overhead, 85%+ gross margin. Provide dedicated executive sponsorship and multi-year expansion roadmaps.
2. High-Maintenance Whales: Large contract size, but burdened by heavy services overhead. Standardize workflows and re-align pricing at renewal.
3. Efficient Core: Mid-sized predictable accounts that onboard seamlessly and compound steady cash flow.
4. Capital Bleeders: Low revenue, high bespoke customization, negative net operating margin. Transition to self-serve, increase contract fees, or gracefully offboard.

Growth is not just about acquiring accounts. It is about acquiring profitable accounts.

Do you know which quadrant your largest customer lives in?
