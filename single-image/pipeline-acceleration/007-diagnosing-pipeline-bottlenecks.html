When an enterprise misses its quarterly revenue forecast, leaders rarely know the real root cause.

They usually blame lead generation or rep closing skills. But when you inspect the CRM telemetry, the breakdown is almost always a localized bottleneck:

  • 40% of deals stall between Discovery and Scoping because the rep failed to quantify the cost of inaction.
  • Another 30% die between Proposal and Procurement because nobody engaged the CFO or InfoSec team early in the cycle.
  • Deals linger in 'Stage 3' for 90+ days without clear next steps, turning into ghost pipeline that ruins forecasting accuracy.

Pipeline bottlenecks are engineering problems. You cannot solve them with pep talks.

You solve them by diagnosing stage conversion velocity, setting strict exit criteria, and deploying targeted playbooks at the exact point of friction.
