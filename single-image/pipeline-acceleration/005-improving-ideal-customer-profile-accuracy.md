## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

Defining your Ideal Customer Profile as 'B2B Software Companies with 100–500 employees' is why your sales cycles take 9 months and churn remains high.

Firmographics only tell you who a company is on paper. They tell you nothing about whether they can profitably buy and retain your platform.

High-growth commercial engines define ICP across four interconnected layers:

1. Firmographic Foundations: Revenue bands, headcount thresholds, and regional compliance standards.
2. Technographic Environment: The existing tools, data stacks, and architecture they have deployed—which determine whether onboarding takes 2 weeks or 6 months.
3. Acute Operational Triggers: Measurable business friction—such as regulatory audit mandates, sudden revenue team turnover, or post-acquisition tech consolidation.
4. Commercial Economics: Gross margin contribution, anticipated cost-to-serve, and verified multi-year expansion potential.

When your sales and marketing teams align on Tier-4 commercial fit, you eliminate deals that drain engineering resources and focus 100% of your energy on high-margin compounders.

How deep does your current ICP definition go?
