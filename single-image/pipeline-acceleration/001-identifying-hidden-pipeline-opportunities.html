Most B2B revenue teams believe they have a demand generation problem.

In 80% of enterprise audits, they actually have a pipeline discovery blind spot.

While marketing continues pouring budget into top-of-funnel acquisition, massive pools of high-margin revenue sit completely dormant in their existing ecosystem:

  1. Dormant High-Fit Accounts: Tier-1 accounts that stalled 6–12 months ago because of budget timing or executive reshuffles, now left untouched in the CRM.
  2. Dark-Funnel Intent Surges: Qualified accounts aggressively researching your exact category and comparison pages without ever filling out a form.
  3. Uncaptured Tier Expansion: Existing enterprise accounts running up against operational ceilings whose adjacent divisions are actively buying point solutions.
  4. Overlooked Micro-Niches: High-yield vertical sub-segments that incumbents ignore because their go-to-market motions are too rigid.

Revenue Acceleration does not require doubling your outreach volume. It requires engineering telemetry that surfaces these four profit pools before your competitors even know an opportunity exists.

How often does your revenue operations team run a systematic dormant-account discovery audit?
