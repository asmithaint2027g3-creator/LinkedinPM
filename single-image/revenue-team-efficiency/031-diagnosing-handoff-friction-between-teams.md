## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

In most B2B enterprises, deals don't die inside meetings. They die in the handoffs between meetings.

When an opportunity moves from Marketing to SDR, from SDR to AE, from AE to Solutions, and from Sales to Customer Success:
- Critical customer context is lost.
- The buyer is forced to repeat their challenges four separate times.
- Internal approval delays add weeks of dead time to the sales cycle.

Every internal handoff is an operational friction point where deals lose momentum and buyers question your operational competence.

To build an efficient revenue engine, RevOps leaders must audit four core transition zones:

1. Marketing to Inbound SDR: Eliminate manual routing queues. Implement autonomous enrichment and instant routing within 120 seconds.
2. SDR to AE Handoff: Ban cold calendar invites. The AE must receive a comprehensive discovery summary so the buyer never starts from scratch.
3. AE to Solutions Architect: Pre-engineer technical scoping templates so engineers enter calls with full architectural clarity.
4. Sales to Customer Success: Automatically synthesize signed commercial agreements into 90-day onboarding milestones.

When internal handoffs are frictionless, deal velocity compounds and customer lifetime value expands.
