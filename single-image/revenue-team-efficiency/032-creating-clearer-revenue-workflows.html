Your CRM data is decaying by approximately 2.5% every single month.

Over the course of a single year, nearly 30% of your customer records become obsolete:
- Executive champions switch companies.
- Target accounts merge, acquire, or restructure divisions.
- Email addresses bounce, and phone numbers disconnect.

When sales reps work with decaying data:
- They spend hours calling disconnected numbers and chasing departed champions.
- Marketing campaigns land in spam folders, destroying domain reputation.
- Executive forecasts rely on outdated pipeline values that never materialize.

Hoping reps will manually update CRM fields on Friday afternoon is not a data strategy.

Modern revenue architectures implement an Autonomous Self-Healing Data Loop:
- Continuous Telemetry: Systems continuously track stakeholder moves and corporate news in real time.
- Automated Data Cleansing: Contacts are algorithmically verified for deliverability without rep effort.
- Relationship Mapping: When a champion moves to a new high-fit account, an automated opportunity signal is immediately triggered.

Clean CRM telemetry is not an administrative luxury. It is the fuel of an AI-native revenue engine.
