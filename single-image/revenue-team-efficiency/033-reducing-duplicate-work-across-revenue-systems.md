## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

Customer retention does not begin when a deal is closed won. It begins during the very first sales call.

When sales reps make promises that Customer Success cannot deliver:
- The customer feels misled before onboarding even begins.
- Implementation drags out, extending time-to-value by months.
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR) drops, and churn spikes at month 12.

High-yield revenue engines connect the entire commercial lifecycle into a single unified telemetry chain:

1. Marketing to Sales: The account arrives with a pre-validated dossier of dark intent and industry catalysts.
2. Sales to Solutions: The technical evaluation is anchored strictly to the quantified commercial pain documented in discovery.
3. Sales to Customer Success: The signed commercial contract automatically generates an executive onboarding charter with agreed milestone dates.
4. Customer Success to Expansion: Product usage telemetry and value realization triggers alert sales when the account is ready for tier expansion.

When your customer journey is a connected system rather than a series of disconnected handoffs, customer retention becomes predictable.
