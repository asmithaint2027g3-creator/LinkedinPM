Over the past five years, B2B companies bought a separate SaaS tool for every single sales problem:
- One tool for email sequencing.
- Another for call recording.
- Another for LinkedIn scraping.
- Another for intent data.
- Another for deal inspection.
- Another for mutual action plans.

The result is pure operational chaos:
- Companies spend $1,200 to $1,800 per rep every month on software licenses.

The era of software point-solution sprawl is over.

Modern enterprise leaders are aggressively consolidating their revenue tech stacks:
- Replacing 10 disconnected tools with a unified AI-native operating layer.

More tools do not create more revenue. Integrated architecture creates revenue.
