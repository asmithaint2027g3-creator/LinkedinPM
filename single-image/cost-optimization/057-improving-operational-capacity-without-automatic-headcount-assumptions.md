## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

The fastest way to destroy sales efficiency is to deploy a $250,000 enterprise account executive to close a $5,000 contract.

Yet many B2B companies force every inbound lead through the exact same 4-step sales process:
- SDR qualifies.
- AE demos.
- AE follows up 5 times.
- AE negotiates contract.

When you calculate the fully loaded labor cost, the company spent $6,000 in sales payroll to close a $5,000 deal. The unit economics are underwater on day one.

Modern revenue architectures deploy an Intelligent Commercial Routing Engine:

1. Low-Complexity Tier (<$15K ACV): Frictionless digital self-serve. Instant sandbox access, transparent pricing, and automated onboarding. Customer acquisition cost: <$500.
2. Mid-Market Tier ($15K–$50K ACV): Sales-assisted digital. Single high-impact consultative session powered by autonomous dossiers, closing within 14 days.
3. Enterprise Tier (>$50K ACV): Dedicated high-touch sales execution. Multi-threaded buying committee mapping, bespoke ROI business cases, and executive alignment.

Match your sales resources to customer unit economics.
