When an enterprise deal takes 9 months to close instead of 3 months, you haven't just delayed revenue.

You have permanently destroyed sales capacity and burned working capital.

Consider the compounding financial cost of slow sales cycles:
- Rep Capacity Destruction: If an AE's deals take 9 months to close, they can manage at most 10 to 12 active opportunities simultaneously. If deals close in 3 months, their annual throughput triples.
- Escalating CAC: Every additional month a deal lingers in the pipeline requires more follow-up hours, more solutions engineering demos, and more executive travel—inflating your acquisition cost.
- Deal Slip Risk: The longer an enterprise deal stays open, the higher the probability of an executive sponsor leaving, an internal budget freeze, or a competitor intervening.

The Math of Sales Velocity:
- Reducing sales cycle duration from 210 days to 90 days increases annual revenue capacity by nearly 2.5x without hiring a single new sales rep.

Sales acceleration is not just a closing metric. It is one of the most powerful financial levers in corporate finance.
