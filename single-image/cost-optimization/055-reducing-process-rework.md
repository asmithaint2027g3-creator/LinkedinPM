## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

Over the past three years, corporate software budgets expanded unchecked.

Every time a sales leader had an operational problem, they purchased another SaaS subscription:
- Today, the average enterprise revenue tech stack contains 14 distinct software tools.
- Over 35% of those licenses sit completely unused (shelfware).
- Another 25% duplicate functionality already available in the core CRM or platform.

When CFOs conduct an objective Stack Rationalization Audit, they discover massive capital waste:

The 4-Category Stack Audit:
1. Core Operating Spine: Foundational systems (CRM, primary database) that are non-negotiable. Ensure deep integration.
2. High-Yield Intelligence: Tools that directly accelerate rep capacity (autonomous dossiers, real-time intent telemetry). Maximize adoption.
3. Redundant Point Tools: Niche tools that solve a minor problem but create data silos and duplicate licensing costs. Consolidate into the core platform.
4. Shelfware: Fancy dashboards and specialized tools that reps haven't opened in 90 days. Cancel immediately upon renewal.

Rationalizing your revenue stack doesn't just save hundreds of thousands of dollars. It removes tool fatigue and lets your sellers focus on selling.

When was the last time your organization audited actual rep login rates across all sales tools?
