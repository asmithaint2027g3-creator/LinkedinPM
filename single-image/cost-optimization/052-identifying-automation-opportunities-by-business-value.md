## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

In most B2B enterprises, three different departments are doing the exact same work in isolation:

- Marketing conducts market research to build a target account list.
- The SDR conducts account research on LinkedIn before sending an email.
- The AE conducts account research again before the first discovery call.
- The Solutions Architect conducts account research a fourth time before the technical demo.

That is four people being paid to do the exact same research task for a single account.

This operational redundancy exists across the entire go-to-market organization:
- Reps spend hours building custom slide decks because they don't know Marketing already created them.
- Customer Success asks the new client the exact same 20 discovery questions the sales rep asked two months prior.
- Sales, RevOps, and Finance each maintain separate forecasting spreadsheets that never reconcile.

Unified Revenue Operations consolidates redundant workflows onto a single data spine:
- One comprehensive 360° Account Dossier shared across all roles.
- Dynamic asset injection directly into the live deal workflow.
- Single-source forecasting telemetry updated in real time.

Stop paying three people to do the work of one.
