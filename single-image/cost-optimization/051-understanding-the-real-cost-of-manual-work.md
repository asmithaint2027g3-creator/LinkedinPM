## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

When leadership looks at Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), they usually see a blended number: 'Our CAC is $35,000.'

A blended average hides the operational waste that is draining your capital.

To fix your unit economics, you must deconstruct CAC across four specific operational drivers:

1. Top-of-Funnel Media Waste: Paying $200 per click to drive traffic from companies that have zero commercial need or lack budget authority.
2. Sales Development Friction: SDR teams spending 40 hours a week sending unpersonalized emails that generate an effective cost of $3,500 per qualified meeting.
3. AE Capacity Dilution: Six-figure enterprise reps spending 65% of their working hours updating spreadsheets, drafting notes, and doing manual research.
4. Late-Stage Proposal Sunk Costs: Investing 50 hours of sales engineering and solutions architecture into opportunities that should have been disqualified on day one.

When you isolate each cost driver, you don't reduce CAC by slashing marketing budgets. You reduce CAC by eliminating operational friction and accelerating pipeline velocity.

Where in your funnel is your acquisition budget leaking the most capital?
