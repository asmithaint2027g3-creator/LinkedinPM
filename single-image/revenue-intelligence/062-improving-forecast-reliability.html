Pipeline value means nothing without measuring Pipeline Velocity.

A sales pipeline with $10M in opportunities that takes 12 months to convert at a 15% win rate generates exactly:
$4,109 in revenue per day.

A precision pipeline with just $4M in opportunities that closes in 3 months at a 35% win rate generates:
$15,555 in revenue per day.

The second team generates nearly 4x the daily cash flow with less than half the pipeline!

Pipeline Velocity is governed by four variables:
Velocity = (Opportunities × Win Rate % × Average Deal Size $) ÷ Sales Cycle Duration

To accelerate velocity, top RevOps teams audit Stage Dwell Times:
- Discovery Stage: Target dwell: 8 days. If an account lingers past 14 days, the commercial pain is unvalidated.
- Scoping Stage: Target dwell: 14 days. Delays indicate solutions alignment friction.
- Proposal Stage: Target dwell: 12 days. Delays indicate lack of CFO economic sponsorship.
- Legal & Security: Target dwell: 10 days. Delays indicate unprepared compliance documentation.

When you manage dwell times by stage, sales cycle duration compresses and cash velocity surges.
