If your blended company win rate is 22%, that average is lying to you.

When you deconstruct win rates by contract size and customer segment, clear patterns emerge:
- You might have a 45% win rate in mid-market accounts with 100–500 employees.
- And an 8% win rate in Fortune 500 enterprise accounts requiring bespoke compliance.

Yet where do most executive teams push their sales reps?
Straight into the Fortune 500 trap—where reps spend 9 months chasing mega-deals that have a 90% chance of failing.

To build an efficient revenue engine, you must map your Commercial Power Alley:

  1. The Power Alley: Where win rates exceed 35%, sales cycles close in under 60 days, and gross margins remain above 80%. This is your core economic growth engine.
  2. The Expansion Zone: High-potential segments that require minor packaging and scoping refinements to reach power alley efficiency.
  3. The Distraction Trap: Massive logos with low win rates, endless procurement friction, and heavy custom engineering that drains operating capital.
  4. The Automated Tier: Low-ACV accounts that must be serviced via self-checkout rather than expensive human sales reps.

Align your sales capacity with where your product naturally wins.

Do you know the exact boundary of your company's commercial power alley?
