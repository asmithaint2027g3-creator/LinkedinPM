## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

Managing a sales team solely on booked ARR is like driving a car by only looking in the rearview mirror.

Booked ARR, quarterly quota attainment, and customer churn are Lagging Indicators:
- By the time they show up on your executive dashboard, the quarter is already over.
- You cannot fix a missed revenue target after the calendar turns.
- Treating lagging numbers as management tools results in reactive firefighting and constant forecast panic.

Elite revenue organizations steer their business using Leading Behavioral Telemetry:

The Leading Revenue Matrix:
1. Multi-Threaded Stakeholder Depth: Are your reps engaging 3+ executive stakeholders by Stage 2, or are they single-threaded with a non-decision maker?
2. Mutual Action Plan Adherence: What percentage of active pipeline deals have a written, agreed timeline signed by the buyer?
3. Stage Dwell Velocity: How many days has an opportunity spent in scoping compared to the historical median for that deal size?
4. Inbound Intent Response Latency: Is your team responding to high-value intent spikes within minutes, or letting leads sit in queues for hours?

Leading indicators give you the power to intervene while the deal is still alive.

What percentage of your weekly executive sales review is spent discussing leading indicators?
