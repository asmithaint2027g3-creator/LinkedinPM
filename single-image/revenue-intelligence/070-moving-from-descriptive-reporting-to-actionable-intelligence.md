## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

Most executive revenue dashboards are completely useless.

They show beautiful, colorful charts that answer the question: 'What happened last month?'
- Blue bars showing total lead volume.
- Green lines showing closed ARR.
- Pie charts showing deal distribution.

Leaders look at them, nod politely, and then ask: 'So what do we actually do on Monday morning?'

An Action-Oriented Revenue Intelligence Dashboard does not report historical data. It diagnoses friction and prescribes commercial action:

1. Stage Dwell Alerts: '14 enterprise deals have exceeded the 18-day median dwell time in Scoping. Mandate: Trigger technical scoping review.'
2. Multi-Threading Telemetry: '65% of active Stage 3 opportunities lack verified contact with the economic buyer. Mandate: Require VP-level outreach before proposal dispatch.'
3. Margin Dilution Monitor: 'Average sales discounting increased by 4% this month. Mandate: Enforce Tier-2 Deal Desk trade-off rules.'
4. Expansion Opportunity Triggers: '8 key customer accounts hit 90% seat saturation this week. Mandate: Deploy automated tier upgrade proposals.'

Your dashboard should not be a museum of past achievements. It should be an operating system that tells your team exactly where to intervene to protect revenue.

Does your current sales dashboard report history, or direct action?
