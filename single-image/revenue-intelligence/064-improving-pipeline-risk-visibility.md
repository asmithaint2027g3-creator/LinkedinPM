## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

Traditional marketing lead scoring is completely broken.

It awards points for superficial digital activity:
- Someone visits your blog: +5 points.
- They download an eBook: +10 points.
- They open a marketing email: +5 points.

The system declares the lead 'Sales Ready!'
The SDR calls them, only to discover it was a junior employee conducting competitive research with zero purchasing authority.

In complex B2B enterprise sales, deals are bought by committees, not individuals.

Predictive Account Engagement Scoring evaluates committee-level commercial telemetry:

1. Stakeholder Breadth: Has the deal engaged the economic buyer, technical evaluator, and legal counsel? (Single-threaded deals have an 80% higher failure rate).
2. Communication Velocity: How fast do buyer stakeholders reply to follow-up deliverables? (Response latency under 4 hours indicates high deal momentum).
3. Collaborative Milestones: Has the prospect shared their internal evaluation timeline or submitted their InfoSec requirements?

When engagement scoring reflects multi-stakeholder commitment rather than website clicks, pipeline predictability becomes an exact science.

How many distinct stakeholders are actively engaged in your top 10 enterprise deals?
