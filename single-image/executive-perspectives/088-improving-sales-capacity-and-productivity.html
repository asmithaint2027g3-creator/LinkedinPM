Why do Marketing, Sales, and Customer Success spend half their executive syncs blaming one another?

Because they are incentivized on competing metrics:
- Marketing is evaluated on MQLs: So they flood the CRM with downloadable eBooks and webinar signups.
- Sales is evaluated on Booked ARR: So reps hand out 30% discounts and promise custom features just to hit their quarterly quota.
- Customer Success is evaluated on Retention: So they inherit discounted, mis-qualified accounts that demand custom engineering and churn in Month 9.

When each team hits its individual goal, the business can still burn cash.

High-velocity CROs replace fragmented KPIs with a single overarching commercial metric:

Net Profitable ARR Yield:
(Qualified ICP Pipeline × Commercial Win Rate % × Gross Margin Contribution %) - Fully Loaded CAC

Under this unified model:
- Marketing is only credited for pipeline that meets verified ICP criteria and advances to proposal.
- Sales commission accelerates on contracts that maintain gross margin and cash-flow terms.
- Customer Success feeds real product adoption telemetry back into top-of-funnel targeting.

One metric. One score. Complete commercial alignment.
