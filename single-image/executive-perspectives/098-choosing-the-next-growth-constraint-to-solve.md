## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

When a company isn't growing fast enough, founders often launch 10 disconnected initiatives simultaneously:
- 'Let's redesign the website!'
- 'Let's launch an outbound email campaign!'
- 'Let's hire an outbound SDR agency!'
- 'Let's build a new product feature!'

The team works 70 hours a week, but revenue doesn't move.

Why? Because according to the Theory of Constraints:
In any complex system, there is only ONE primary constraint limiting throughput at any given moment.

Optimizing a non-constraint produces zero measurable results:
- If your constraint is deal conversion at the proposal stage, generating 500 more marketing leads will not produce a single dollar of additional revenue. It will just waste rep time.
- If your constraint is post-sale customer retention, doubling your sales headcount will only accelerate your churn rate.

Apply the Growth Constraint Triage:
1. Demand Constraint: Are your reps' calendars under 50% booked with qualified buyers? -> Fix: Intent discovery and account selection.
2. Conversion Constraint: Are qualified meetings failing to progress to proposal? -> Fix: Consultative discovery and problem-to-value translation.
3. Velocity Constraint: Are deals stalling for 90+ days in procurement? -> Fix: Mutual Action Plans and pre-cleared legal packs.
4. Retention Constraint: Are customers churning after Year 1? -> Fix: Delivery standardization and time-to-value acceleration.

Find your single primary bottleneck. Focus 100% of your executive energy on solving it.

What is the single biggest constraint limiting your revenue growth today?
