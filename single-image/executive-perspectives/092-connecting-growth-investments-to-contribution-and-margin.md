## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

When marketing and sales report customer acquisition cost, they almost always report an artificially low number:
'Our CAC is $14,500.'

How did they calculate that?
Direct ad spend plus rep commission divided by new logos.

That calculation ignores over half of the real capital required to acquire an enterprise customer.

The Fully Loaded CAC Model incorporates all commercial overhead:
- Solutions Engineering: Hundreds of hours spent building custom demonstrations and proof-of-concept sandboxes.
- Legal & Deal Desk Overhead: External legal counsel reviewing custom redlines and non-standard terms.
- RevOps Tech Stack Allocation: The licensing cost of the 14 tools used to prospect, track, and close the deal.
- Executive Sponsorship: C-suite time and travel spent salvaging complex negotiations.

When CFOs model Fully Loaded CAC:
- True acquisition cost is often 2x higher than reported.
- Real cash payback periods stretch from 6 months to 16 months.
- Certain customer segments turn out to have negative lifetime value on a net margin basis.

Measure true CAC. You cannot manage enterprise capital efficiency with partial math.

Does your company calculate direct CAC or fully loaded CAC?
