## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

In the early days of a B2B startup, founder-led sales is a superpower.

Founders have unmatched domain passion, product vision, and the authority to adjust pricing and roadmaps on the fly:
- They close the first 20 enterprise customers through sheer conviction.
- But as the company scales toward $10M ARR, founder-led sales becomes the single biggest bottleneck in the enterprise.

Why? Because founder intuition cannot be cloned into new sales reps:
- New reps don't have the founder's encyclopedic industry knowledge.
- They struggle to navigate complex discovery.
- The founder is dragged into every closing call, working 80-hour weeks while reps miss quota.

To scale, founders must transition from Founder-Led Sales to System-Led Sales:

The 4-Phase Transition Roadmap:
1. Codify Founder Intuition: Document the exact diagnostic questions, commercial catalysts, and customer objection counters the founder uses intuitively.
2. Build the Autonomous Intelligence Layer: Deploy systems that synthesize 360° account dossiers and serve dynamic battlecards to reps automatically.
3. Enforce Objective Commercial Guardrails: Replace founder discretion with clear Deal Desk rules and stage exit criteria.
4. Scale Independent Sales Capacity: Frontline sellers consistently close enterprise contracts without the founder ever stepping into the Zoom room.

True enterprise value is built when the sales process works independently of the founder.

Are you running an enterprise sales organization, or are you still the chief sales rep?
