When board members only ask: 'Did we hit the ARR number this quarter?', they fail to see the structural risks developing inside the enterprise.

A company can hit its quarterly ARR target while its commercial engine is actively breaking down:
- The target was achieved only because the CEO stepped in to close a single massive deal at a 40% discount.
- Meanwhile, 80% of frontline reps missed their quotas.
- Pipeline generation for next quarter dried up.
- CAC increased by 35%, and gross margins collapsed.

To provide real corporate governance, board members must inspect five structural revenue health metrics:

The Boardroom Revenue Scorecard:
1. Pipeline Coverage Density: Ratio of rigorously qualified pipeline to target quota (Target: 3.0x to 3.5x verified coverage).
2. Frontline Capacity Attainment: Percentage of reps meeting target (Target: 70%+ across the entire team).
3. CAC Payback Velocity: Speed of cash-flow recovery (Target: Sub-12 months on a gross-profit basis).
4. Net Contract Gross Margin: True contract profitability after factoring in discounting and custom delivery (Target: 80%+).
5. Forecast Variance: Discrepancy between executive commit and closed revenue (Target: Under 5%).

When boards inspect systems rather than celebrating lucky outcomes, revenue predictability compounds.
