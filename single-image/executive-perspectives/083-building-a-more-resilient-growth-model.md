## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

In corporate finance, pursuing one financial metric while ignoring the other two is a recipe for value destruction:

- Pursuing Growth without Margin creates an operational monster: You sign high-revenue contracts that require armies of support engineers, crushing your cash flow.
- Pursuing Margin without Growth leads to stagnation: You protect profitability but surrender market share to aggressive competitors.
- Pursuing Growth without Capital Efficiency creates a burn machine that dilutes equity and collapses when funding environments tighten.

Top-decile CEOs manage the Golden Triangle of Enterprise Valuation:

1. Revenue Acceleration: Finding attractive market whitespace, engineering qualified demand, and compressing sales cycle velocity.
2. Margin Expansion: Enforcing deal desk discipline, eliminating bespoke scope creep, and automating customer delivery to keep gross margins above 85%.
3. Capital Discipline: Keeping CAC payback under 12 months, consolidating redundant software, and multiplying rep selling productivity rather than adding headcount.

When all three pillars operate in synchronized harmony, your company generates superior operating leverage and commands the highest valuation multiples in its category.

How balanced is your company's revenue triangle today?
