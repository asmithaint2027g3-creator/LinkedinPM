When the CEO asks: 'How is the business performing?', they usually get four completely different answers:
- The CMO says: 'Marketing is crushing it! MQLs are up 45%.'
- The CRO says: 'Sales is struggling because lead quality is terrible.'
- The CFO says: 'CAC payback is blowing out and discounts are too high.'
- The CPO says: 'Customers love the new product features.'

When executive leaders operate with disconnected departmental scorecards, they spend their executive committee meetings arguing about whose data is accurate.

The CEO's mandate is to eliminate departmental silos and enforce a Unified Revenue Spine:

  1. Chief Executive Officer: Steers capital efficiency, enterprise valuation multiple, and Rule of 40 performance.
  2. Chief Revenue Officer: Drives pipeline velocity, rep quota attainment, and commercial win-rate science.
  3. Chief Financial Officer: Enforces CAC payback velocity, gross margin preservation, and forecast predictability.
  4. Chief Product Officer: Aligns product roadmap priorities with verified commercial customer value realization.

When the entire executive team looks at the exact same empirical telemetry, finger-pointing stops and synchronized commercial execution begins.

How aligned is your executive committee on your company's core commercial telemetry?
