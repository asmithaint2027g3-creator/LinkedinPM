## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

In corporate finance, price is the single most powerful lever of operating profit.

A 1% improvement in price realization generates an 8% to 11% increase in operating profit—far more than cutting operating expenses or increasing sales volume.

Yet in most companies, pricing decisions are made in the final 48 hours of the quarter by stressed sales reps desperate to hit quota.

The CFO must establish an unyielding Pricing Governance Architecture:

1. Absolute Gross Margin Floor: No contract may be executed below a 75% gross margin contribution without formal CFO and CEO sign-off.
2. The Reciprocal Trade-Off Rule: Banning one-way discounts. If procurement demands a 10% price reduction, sales must demand an annual upfront payment or a 2-year commitment.
3. Delivery SOW Boundaries: All bespoke integrations and custom reports must be billed as paid professional services, never bundled into 'free onboarding.'
4. Contractual Annual Escalators: Standard language embedding a 5% to 7% price increase on contract anniversary dates to protect margins against inflation.

When pricing is governed with financial discipline, gross margins expand and corporate free cash flow surges.
