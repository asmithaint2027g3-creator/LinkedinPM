Why does it take five business days for an enterprise sales rep to deliver a proposal after finishing a discovery call?

The typical proposal workflow is a bureaucratic nightmare:
- The rep copies an old proposal deck from a different customer.
- They forget to update the company name on slide four.
- They wait two days for Deal Desk to approve standard pricing.
- They wait another two days for legal to generate a clean Master Services Agreement (MSA).

By the time the buyer receives the proposal, the excitement from the discovery call has completely dissipated.

Autonomous Proposal & Contract Generation cuts turnaround time from five days to 15 minutes:

1. Telemetry Ingestion: The system pulls validated discovery data—customer problem, quantified cost of inaction, target user counts, and technical requirements.
2. Pre-Engineered Assembly: Generates a tailored, board-ready business case deck with peer ROI models and implementation milestones.
3. Pre-Approved Legal Packaging: Automatically generates the standard MSA and security schedule using pre-approved company guardrails.

The rep reviews the documents, adds strategic executive nuances, and delivers a polished commercial package while the conversation is still fresh in the buyer's mind.
