Recording your sales calls is not an AI strategy.

Almost every B2B company today records Zoom meetings. But what happens to those recordings?
- They sit in an online folder that nobody has time to re-watch.
- The rep still spends 20 minutes typing fragmented notes into the CRM.
- Important buyer objections and agreed deadlines remain buried in an hour of unstructured video.

Passive recording creates a digital landfill.

True Meeting Intelligence transforms unstructured conversational audio into structured commercial execution:

  1. Entity & Milestone Extraction: Automatically isolates the buyer's stated P&L metrics, mentioned competitors, and agreed internal milestones.
  2. Zero-Click CRM Synchronization: Updates opportunity stage, closes out tasks, and logs next action dates without the rep touching a keyboard.
  3. Pre-Drafted Board-Ready Memos: Generates a tailored executive follow-up memo in the rep's inbox within 3 minutes of call completion.
  4. Coachable Behavioral Telemetry: Automatically scores talk-to-listen ratios, objection handling efficacy, and executive multi-threading.

Stop storing video files. Start extracting actionable revenue telemetry.
