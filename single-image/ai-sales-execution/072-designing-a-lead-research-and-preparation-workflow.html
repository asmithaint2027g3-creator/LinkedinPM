The most stressful moment in enterprise selling is when a buyer raises an unexpected objection or mentions a competitor you didn't prepare for.

In the old model:
- The rep stumbles, gets defensive, or promises to 'check with the team and circle back.'
- The buyer senses hesitation.
- The momentum of the deal is permanently broken.

With In-Flight Real-Time Deal Guidance:
- As the prospect mentions an architectural hurdle or competitor pricing claim, conversation intelligence analyzes the context in real time.
- A discrete guidance card surfaces on the seller's second screen:
- Exact competitive differentiation points.
- Tailored diagnostic questions that expose the competitor's hidden implementation costs.
- Relevant customer case studies that resolved the exact same concern.

The seller remains calm, consultative, and completely in control.

Real-time guidance doesn't replace the rep's talent. It ensures they never have a bad meeting.

How does your team arm sellers when unexpected objections hit during live calls?
