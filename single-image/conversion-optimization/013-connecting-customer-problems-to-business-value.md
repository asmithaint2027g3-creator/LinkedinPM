## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

If you pitch software features to an executive, they will evaluate you as an expense.

If you connect their operational bottlenecks to balance sheet impact, they will evaluate you as a high-yield strategic investment.

The biggest mistake B2B sales teams make is stopping at Level 1 pain:
- The user tells the rep: 'Our CRM data is disorganized and manual updates take too much time.'
- The rep immediately demos their automated workflow features.
- The CFO rejects the purchase order because 'improving data entry' is not a top-three strategic board objective.

To win enterprise deals, you must cross the Problem-to-Value Bridge:

1. Functional Pain: Manual data logging and fragmented account research.
2. Operational Bottleneck: Sales development velocity is constrained; response latency averages 6 hours.
3. Commercial Consequence: Inbound conversion decays by 40%, generating $1.2M in annual pipeline leakage.
4. Executive Outcome: Quota attainment drops, CAC increases by 25%, and EBITDA margins contract.

Now the conversation is not about software seats. It is about recovering $1.2M in leaked revenue and expanding enterprise margins.

Which level of value is your sales team pitching today?
