A stalled deal is not a neutral asset in your CRM. It is an active drain on your sales capacity.

Every week an account executive spends preparing follow-up emails, forecasting close dates, and agonizing over an unresponsive prospect is time stolen from active opportunities:

When deals stall, there are only four real causes:

  1. Lack of Economic Buyer Alignment: Your champion is excited, but they lack the budget authority to sign a purchase order without CFO sign-off.
  2. The Business Case Is Too Weak: The problem was framed as an operational convenience rather than an urgent financial necessity.
  3. Internal Political Resistance: Another internal department views your platform as a threat to their budget or headcount.
  4. Ghost Pipeline: The prospect has chosen another vendor or decided to do nothing, but avoids telling you to avoid conflict.

Apply strict triage rules: If an opportunity fails to provide verified buyer actions for 30 days, send a respectful close-out notice and re-allocate rep capacity to high-momentum accounts.

Cleaning dead deals out of your pipeline is the fastest way to increase real sales velocity.
