## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

B2B buyers do not hate buying. They hate the friction vendor sales processes force them to endure.

Consider the typical enterprise buyer journey:
- They fill out a 15-field form on your website.
- An SDR calls them 24 hours later to ask the exact same questions they just typed into the form.
- They are transferred to an AE who makes them repeat their business challenges from scratch.
- The AE refuses to discuss pricing until the third meeting.
- Procurement and legal are surprised by non-standard contracts in week twelve.

Every friction point gives your buyer an opportunity to pause, re-evaluate, or do nothing.

Modern AI-Native revenue teams treat buying friction as an existential revenue leak:
- Automated enrichment replaces long inquiry forms.
- Synchronized account dossiers ensure no prospect ever repeats their story twice.
- Transparent value modeling provides clear commercial expectations early.
- Pre-packaged InfoSec and compliance documentation speeds legal approval.

Make it frictionless for qualified buyers to buy from you.

Where is the biggest friction point in your current sales journey?
