## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

When an enterprise deal is marked 'Closed Lost' in the CRM, 70% of reps write down: 'Lost to competitor' or 'Lost on price.'

In reality, price is almost never the real reason.

Extensive commercial post-mortems reveal that conversion losses occur because of systemic execution gaps earlier in the sales cycle:

1. Post-Discovery Drop-Off: The prospect goes silent because the rep pitched features before the prospect agreed on the quantified cost of doing nothing.
2. Post-Demonstration Stall: The buyer says 'this looks great, let me socialize it internally'—a polite signal that you failed to provide a defensible business case for the CFO.
3. Severe Price Resistance: The buyer aggressively demands a 30% discount because the proposal reads like a technical spec rather than a strategic business transformation.
4. Late-Stage Procurement Veto: InfoSec or procurement halts the deal because the rep failed to engage legal and compliance stakeholders early.

Stop accepting 'timing' as an excuse for lost deals. Treat conversion losses as diagnostic signals that reveal exactly where your sales methodology needs re-engineering.
