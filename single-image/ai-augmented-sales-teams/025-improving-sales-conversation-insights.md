## Complete LinkedIn Post & Caption

When an enterprise deal advances to Stage 3, who actually understands what is happening?

In most companies:
- The AE has notes scribbled in a personal notebook.
- The Solutions Architect asks the customer the same technical questions on demo day.
- The Sales VP has to interrogate the rep for 20 minutes during pipeline reviews to understand if the deal is real.
- When the AE goes on vacation, the deal freezes.

Knowledge silos within your own revenue team kill deal velocity.

A modern revenue engine maintains a living, continuously updated Opportunity Brief:

1. Commercial Foundations: The exact quantified problem, verified business cost of delay, and the executive who owns the P&L impact.
2. Committee Landscape: A mapped org chart showing champions, blockers, and who has signed off on the business case.
3. Technical Architecture: Precise API, compliance, and integration requirements documented without forcing the buyer to repeat themselves.
4. Mutual Milestones: A shared timeline agreed upon by both vendor and customer leading to contract execution.

When your entire revenue team operates from a single source of deal truth, handoffs are seamless and win rates soar.
