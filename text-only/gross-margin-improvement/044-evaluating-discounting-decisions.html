The fastest way to turn an 80% software margin into a 35% services margin is 'unlimited onboarding support.'

During high-stakes enterprise sales cycles, reps frequently make verbal concessions to push contracts over the finish line:
- 'Don't worry, our team will help you build all the custom dashboard reports.'
- 'We will handle the data migration from your legacy database free of charge.'
- 'Our solutions engineers will write the custom API scripts for you.'

When the contract is signed:
- Customer Success inherits dozens of undocumented commitments.
- Onboarding timeline stretches from 30 days to 6 months.
- High-priced engineers spend hundreds of hours doing bespoke services work that was never factored into the commercial model.

Scope creep is a systemic failure of commercial governance:

  1. Pre-Engineered SOW Guardrails: Statements of Work must feature explicit boundary clauses detailing exactly what is included and what constitutes a billable change order.
  2. Modular Implementation Packages: Standardize onboarding into fixed tiers (e.g., Core vs Enterprise Guided) with predefined milestone deliverables.
  3. Commercial Accountability for Sales: If an account executive commits bespoke engineering hours to win a deal, the cost of those hours must be debited against their deal commission.

Protect your delivery teams from open-ended promises. Standardize your scope to protect your gross margin.
