## Complete LinkedIn Post Copy

Show me how you compensate your sales team, and I will show you why your gross margins are eroding.

If an account executive earns the exact same 10% commission on an 85% gross margin software contract as they do on a heavily discounted 45% margin deal loaded with custom engineering, what will they sell?

They will sell whatever takes the least resistance to close:
- They will grant 25% discounts without hesitation.
- They will promise bespoke custom features.
- They will push low-margin services to artificially inflate the booking number.

Reps do not do this maliciously. They are simply behaving in accordance with how they are financially incentivized.

Modern revenue leadership aligns compensation directly with Gross Margin Contribution:

1. Margin-Adjusted Commission Bands: Commission rates accelerate on contracts signed above target gross margin thresholds (e.g., 12% commission on 80%+ margin deals; 4% commission on heavily discounted deals).
2. Multi-Year Cash Multipliers: Higher commission percentages rewarded for contracts secured with annual upfront cash terms, improving corporate working capital.
3. Retention & Churn Clawbacks: If a customer churns within 9 months due to clear ICP mis-qualification, rep commission is partially adjusted.

When sales reps make more money by protecting gross margins than by handing out discounts, pricing discipline becomes second nature.

How does your current sales compensation plan reward contract profitability?
