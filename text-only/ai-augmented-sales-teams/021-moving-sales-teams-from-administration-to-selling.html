If your top enterprise account executives spend 65% of their working hours logging CRM data, formatting slides, and hunting down notes, you are paying six-figure salaries for administrative coordination.

Every major enterprise benchmark confirms the same reality: The average B2B account executive spends less than 35% of their workweek actively engaging buyers.

Where does the other 65% disappear?
- 12 hours/week spent manually updating CRM fields and activity logs.
- 8 hours/week digging through 14 different tools to piece together account research.
- 6 hours/week drafting standard follow-up summaries and internal deal-desk requests.

When sellers are buried in operational overhead:
- Discovery calls become rushed.
- Follow-up responsiveness slows from hours to days.
- Rep quota attainment drops, while executive leadership wonders why pipeline velocity is crawling.

AI-Native Revenue Transformation is not about replacing human relationship builders. It is about deploying autonomous agents that handle data ingestion, pre-meeting briefs, and post-call CRM logging—liberating sellers to do what only humans can do: build trust, navigate organizational politics, and negotiate complex business outcomes.

Give your sellers their calendars back.
