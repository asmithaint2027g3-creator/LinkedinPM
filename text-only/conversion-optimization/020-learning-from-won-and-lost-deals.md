## Complete LinkedIn Post Copy

If your win/loss analysis consists of reading CRM notes written by sales reps, your data is fundamentally biased.

When reps win: 'They loved our product, our team executed flawlessly, and our vision won the day.'
When reps lose: 'Competitor slashed their price by 50%' or 'They had an internal budget freeze.'

Human nature makes self-reported sales data notoriously unreliable. To extract actionable intelligence, organizations must build a structured, third-party win/loss review cadence:

Inspect three critical commercial dimensions across every major outcome:

1. The True Buying Trigger: What specific catalytic event prompted the buyer to look for a solution in the first place? If you won, did your messaging reflect that trigger? If you lost, did the competitor address it better?
2. Champion Capital & Internal Consensus: Did your sales team successfully multi-thread into the executive economic buyer, or did they rely entirely on a single non-decision maker?
3. The Value-to-Price Ratio: Did the buyer view your solution as a high-yield transformation investment, or was your proposal compared line-by-line against a lower-priced commodity alternative?

When you review won and lost deals with clinical objectivity, patterns emerge that inform product roadmaps, pricing models, and sales playbooks.

Win/loss analysis is not a post-mortem to assign blame. It is the continuous learning engine of an AI-native revenue system.

When was the last time an unbiased third party interviewed your lost prospects?
