## Complete LinkedIn Post Copy

Filling your sales reps' calendars with low-fit demos is the fastest way to destroy quota capacity.

When sales leadership demands more meetings, marketing often lowers the threshold. The result is predictable:
- Account executives spend 40% of their selling hours educating non-buyers.
- Pipeline figures look inflated on executive dashboards.
- Win rates plummet at the proposal stage.
- Reps miss quota while working 60-hour weeks.

High-velocity revenue teams do not celebrate raw lead volume. They enforce qualification discipline around four distinct commercial pillars:

1. Economic Severity: Does the prospect experience a measurable business cost by doing nothing, or is this an exploratory initiative?
2. Authority & Buying Group Dynamics: Are you speaking with a single evaluator, or do you have a path to the executive who owns the P&L impact?
3. Strategic ICP Alignment: Does this account match your highest-retention customer profile, or will closing them result in custom engineering and heavy customer success drain?
4. Commercial Timing & Execution Window: Is there an agreed event or regulatory deadline driving urgency within the next 90 days?

When qualification is rigorous at the intake stage, your pipeline value shrinks on paper—but your close rates double, sales cycles compress, and customer gross margins expand.

Stop measuring meetings booked. Start measuring qualified pipeline progression.
