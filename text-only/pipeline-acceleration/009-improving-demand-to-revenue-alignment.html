Marketing celebrates hitting 120% of MQL quota. Sales hits 65% of revenue quota. Who is right?

Neither. The business lost.

This recurring friction is the direct consequence of disjointed incentives:
- Marketing is evaluated on top-of-funnel volume: form submissions, webinar signups, and eBook downloads.
- Sales is evaluated on booked annual recurring revenue and commercial cash collection.
- Customer Success is left holding the bag when low-fit customers churn after 9 months.

When departments operate with separate scorecards, each team optimizes its own operational comfort while the enterprise as a whole leaks capital.

In an AI-Native Revenue Transformation, demand generation and sales execution merge into a single continuous workflow:

  1. Unified ICP Scorecards: Marketing is only credited for pipeline that meets verified commercial qualification criteria.
  2. Shared Revenue Quotas: Marketing leadership shares pipeline conversion and closed-won revenue accountability with sales.
  3. Closed-Loop Telemetry: Sales outcome data immediately feeds back into top-of-funnel targeting, automatically suppressing segments that stall or demand heavy discounts.

Stop running two separate go-to-market teams. Build one integrated revenue engine.
