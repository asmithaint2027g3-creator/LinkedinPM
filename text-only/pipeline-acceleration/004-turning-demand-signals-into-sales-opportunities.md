## Complete LinkedIn Post Copy

Buying intent data without context is just expensive noise.

Over the past three years, B2B sales teams have invested heavily in intent feeds, dark funnel trackers, and signal tools. Yet SDR reply rates continue to decline.

The breakdown happens at the interpretation layer:
- A signal only tells you an IP address read three articles on a topic.
- It does not tell you if the reader was an intern conducting university research or a VP of Engineering preparing an RFP.
- Blindly firing 8-step email cadences the second a signal fires alienates high-value buyers.

To turn raw demand signals into predictable enterprise pipeline, top revenue engines apply a Signal Triangulation Framework:

1. Intent Surge: High-frequency research on specific commercial problem areas across third-party networks.
2. ICP Fit Verification: Immediate algorithmic confirmation that the account has the headcount, budget profile, and tech infrastructure required for your solution.
3. Org Movement Telemetry: Leadership additions, board mandates, tech stack sunsets, or job openings that indicate budget has been unlocked.

Only when all three intersect does the account move into active sales engagement. 

Don't spam accounts that show a spark of intent. Build an intelligent value thesis that addresses the exact commercial transition they are undergoing.
