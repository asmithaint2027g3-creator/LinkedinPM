## Complete LinkedIn Post Copy

The single biggest complaint new enterprise customers have is: 'Why do I have to re-explain my business to your onboarding team?'

Think about the buyer's experience:
- They spent 3 months in detailed discovery and technical demos with your sales team.
- They explained their corporate goals, their internal bottlenecks, and their IT requirements.
- The contract is signed.
- Two weeks later, a Customer Success Manager jumps on a kickoff call and asks: 'So, tell me what you're hoping to achieve with our platform.'

In that single moment, the customer's confidence in your organization plummets.

Autonomous Post-Sale Handoffs eliminate internal amnesia:

The Connected Handoff Architecture:
1. Automated Dossier Synthesis: The moment the contract is signed in DocuSign, autonomous intelligence parses all recorded sales calls, discovery notes, and technical scoping documents.
2. Customer Success Onboarding Charter: Generates a tailored 90-day onboarding charter detailing:
   - The customer's primary economic objectives and validated P&L metrics.
   - Key internal stakeholders, champions, and potential skeptics.
   - Identified IT and integration dependencies.
3. Contextual First Kickoff: The CSM enters the kickoff call already fully briefed: 'We know your top priority is reducing response latency by Q3. Here is our exact 30-day plan to deliver that outcome.'

When your post-sale team operates with seamless context, customer time-to-value accelerates and renewals become guaranteed.
