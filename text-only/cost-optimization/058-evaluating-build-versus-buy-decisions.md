## Complete LinkedIn Post Copy

Carrying 100 dead opportunities in your CRM isn't harmless optimism. It is an active financial drain on quota capacity.

Every sales organization has a pipeline graveyard:
- Opportunities that haven't answered an email in 45 days.
- Prospects who said 'check back with us next quarter' six months ago.
- Deals where the champion left the company, but the rep refuses to mark it closed-lost.

Reps hold onto bad pipeline because an empty pipeline feels terrifying.

But the opportunity cost is devastating:
- Every hour spent preparing follow-up emails for a dead prospect is an hour stolen from finding active, high-intent buyers.
- Managers waste 30% of weekly pipeline inspections debating fictional opportunities.
- Executive forecasts become unmoored from reality, leading to surprise revenue misses at quarter-end.

Ruthless disqualification is a superpower of high-performing revenue engines:
- If a prospect refuses to introduce the economic buyer after two meetings, disqualify.
- If there is no agreed deadline driving action in the next 90 days, disqualify.
- If the customer does not have the technical infrastructure to succeed with your product, disqualify.

Disqualifying bad pipeline on Day 14 doesn't hurt your quota. It liberates your time to close high-yield deals on Day 45.

Purge the ghost pipeline from your CRM.

What percentage of your active opportunities took a verified buyer action in the last 7 days?
