## Complete LinkedIn Post Copy

A marketing channel that generates leads for $40 is a financial disaster if those leads convert to revenue at 0.5%.

Most marketing attribution reports focus on cost-per-lead (CPL):
- The paid social agency boasts: 'We drove 800 leads this month at an average cost of $42!'
- The marketing team presents a slide full of green metrics.
- Meanwhile, the sales team complains that none of those 800 leads have a real budget or authority to purchase.

When you audit channels on Closed-Won ARR and Cost of Customer Acquisition (CAC):
- Channel A (Paid Social): $42 CPL -> 0.6% conversion -> $38,000 blended CAC -> 22-month payback.
- Channel B (Executive Intent & Content): $350 CPL -> 18% conversion -> $11,000 blended CAC -> 4-month payback.

Channel B looks eight times more expensive on paper. In commercial reality, it is more than three times more capital efficient.

High-yield revenue leaders manage marketing budgets on Closed-Won Unit Economics:
1. Stage Progression Yield: How quickly does channel volume advance from Stage 1 to Proposal?
2. Average Contract Value (ACV) Variance: Do certain channels systematically produce customers that demand heavy discounts?
3. Gross Margin & Retention by Channel: Do customers acquired through specific campaigns churn at higher rates after year one?

Stop managing marketing for lead volume. Steer your demand budget based on cash-flow payback velocity.

Which of your current acquisition channels is actually generating profitable revenue?
