## Complete LinkedIn Post Copy

When a company needs to double revenue, the default executive response is: 'We need to double our sales headcount.'

This is the most expensive mistake in B2B.

Consider what actually happens when you double sales rep headcount:
- Management layers double, increasing corporate overhead.
- Existing sales territories are carved into smaller, less viable fragments.
- New reps take 6 to 9 months to ramp, consuming cash without producing revenue.
- Culture dilutes, communication friction increases, and quota attainment across the team drops from 65% to 40%.

High-performance revenue engines do not scale linearly through headcount. They scale through Headcount Productivity:

Instead of hiring 20 new account executives at $250K fully loaded each ($5M annual payroll commitment):
- Deploy autonomous intelligence that absorbs 65% of existing reps' administrative tasks.
- Provide pre-synthesized 360° dossiers that cut prep time from 45 minutes to 3 minutes.
- Automate CRM hygiene and post-call follow-ups.

When existing reps spend 70% of their week in active customer conversations instead of 30%, your current team can generate 2x the pipeline and revenue—without adding a single dollar in base payroll.

Fix rep productivity before you expand headcount.

What percentage of your current sales team is consistently hitting quota today?
