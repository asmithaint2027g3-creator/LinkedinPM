B2B companies don't lose revenue because competitors have better products. They lose revenue through silent operational leaks in their funnel.

Revenue leakage is the hidden capital bleed of enterprise go-to-market:
- Marketing spends $100K driving intent that is never routed to a rep.
- Reps hold meetings with non-decision makers who lack budget.
- Commercial proposals are sent without an agreed business case, only to be rejected by the CFO.
- Customers churn after year one because sales over-promised features during closing.

In an enterprise audit, revenue leakage concentrates around four friction points:

  1. Inbound Routing Leakage: Leads sitting unworked for 24+ hours, resulting in a 70%+ drop in contact rates.
  2. Discovery Qualification Leakage: Advancing exploratory conversations into active pipeline stages without verifying budget or business consequences.
  3. Proposal Commoditization Leakage: Submitting technical pricing sheets rather than executive business cases, triggering 30%+ discounting pressure.
  4. Handoff Context Leakage: Forcing Customer Success to restart discovery from scratch, destroying first-year time-to-value.

When you plug these four operational leaks, you don't need to spend more money on customer acquisition to grow revenue.

Audit your pipeline for silent leaks. The fastest way to accelerate revenue is to stop leaking what you already generated.
