## Complete LinkedIn Post Copy

When a customer tells you at renewal 'We've decided not to renew,' they didn't make that decision yesterday. They made it 4 months ago.

Customer churn is rarely sudden. It is a slow, observable decay:
- The executive champion who bought the platform leaves the company.
- Weekly active user logins decline by 20% over consecutive weeks.
- The customer stops attending scheduled quarterly business reviews.
- Support tickets shift from strategic questions to basic password resets.

If your Customer Success team only looks at account health 30 days before renewal, it is already too late to intervene.

Predictive Churn Telemetry looks for organizational and behavioral friction 90 to 120 days in advance:

1. Champion Departure Telemetry: Continuous monitoring of LinkedIn profiles to detect when your primary economic sponsor changes jobs.
2. License Dormancy Ratios: Tracking when paid licenses sit unassigned or inactive for more than 21 days.
3. Unaddressed Technical Blockers: Tracking recurring support tickets that indicate user frustration with core workflows.
4. Executive Disengagement: Flagging accounts where senior leadership has not interacted with customer success in over 60 days.

When churn risk is detected at Day 90 instead of Day 330, leadership can deploy an executive sponsor, conduct an emergency value audit, and rescue the account before the cancellation letter is drafted.

What early warning triggers does your team track to detect silent customer churn?
