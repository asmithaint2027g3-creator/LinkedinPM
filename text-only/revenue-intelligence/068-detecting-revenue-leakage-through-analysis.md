## Complete LinkedIn Post Copy

Waiting for the annual contract renewal to pitch account expansion is why your net revenue retention is plateauing.

Top-tier software enterprises don't wait for Month 11 to discuss expansion:
- By Month 11, the customer's budget for the following fiscal year is already locked.
- If you spring an expansion pitch on them during renewal, they view it as an aggressive price hike.
- The conversation becomes adversarial and defensive.

Compounding revenue engines expand accounts continuously using Behavioral Product Telemetry:

Four Product Signals That Indicate Commercial Expansion Readiness:
1. License & Seat Saturation: The account has reached 85%+ active seat allocation 4 months ahead of schedule.
2. Cross-Departmental Logins: Users with email domains from adjacent divisions or subsidiaries begin accessing shared workspaces.
3. API & Data Query Surges: Consumption of data connectors or core workflows spikes 3x above their initial contract threshold.
4. Executive Feature Engagement: Senior C-suite stakeholders begin logging in directly to inspect high-level analytics dashboards.

When product telemetry alerts the account team to these milestones in real time, the expansion conversation is natural and consultative:
'We noticed your operations team in Europe just activated the platform. Let's look at enterprise tiering to give them dedicated support.'

Don't wait for renewals. Expand accounts at the moment of verified customer value realization.

How does your sales team currently track product usage signals in existing accounts?
