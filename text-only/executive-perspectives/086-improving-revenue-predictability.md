## Complete LinkedIn Post Copy

What got your company to $10M in ARR will break your company on the road to $50M.

Between $1M and $10M in ARR, growth is driven by sheer human hustle:
- Founders jump on every closing call.
- Early reps memorize customer nuances and compensate for broken software.
- Disorganized CRM data doesn't matter because everyone sits in the same room.

Between $10M and $50M, that hustle model violently breaks down:
- Founder capacity is exhausted.
- New reps don't have the context of early hires and fail to hit quota.
- Hand-offs between Marketing, Sales, and Customer Success turn into blame games.
- Deals slip unpredictably, discounting explodes, and customer churn spikes.

Revenue engines break at scale because companies try to scale an un-engineered process:

How Modern CROs Build Scalable Revenue Infrastructure:
1. Standardize Qualification Gates: Disqualify bad-fit leads algorithmically before they waste sales capacity.
2. Automate Deal Intelligence: Equip every new rep with autonomous account dossiers so they perform like 5-year veterans on Day 30.
3. Establish Single-Source Telemetry: Connect marketing intent, sales stage velocity, and customer onboarding milestones into one unified data spine.

Scaling is not about working harder. It is about replacing individual heroics with disciplined systems architecture.

Is your commercial engine built to scale, or running on pure rep hustle?
