If your sales team has a software tool for every day of the week, your GTM budget is leaking hundreds of thousands of dollars.

During the zero-interest-rate boom, software budgets were completely ungoverned:
- Sales managers put niche tools on corporate credit cards.
- Marketing bought dedicated analytics suites that were abandoned after 60 days.
- Today, the average enterprise GTM team uses 12 to 16 distinct software platforms, costing $1,200 to $1,800 per rep per month.

The CFO's mandate is to enforce SaaS Stack Rationalization:

  1. Zero-Based Software Auditing: Require every software contract up for renewal to justify its existence based on verified rep adoption and direct revenue attribution. (If login rates are below 50%, terminate the contract).
  2. Eliminate Redundant Capabilities: Consolidate overlapping point solutions (e.g., separate tools for sequencing, conversation recording, and intent data) into a unified platform.
  3. Consolidate Seat Licensing: Move away from disconnected user licenses toward platform-wide enterprise agreements that reduce cost per employee by 40%+.

Cutting software sprawl doesn't just save money. It removes operational friction and lets your sellers focus on customer conversations.
