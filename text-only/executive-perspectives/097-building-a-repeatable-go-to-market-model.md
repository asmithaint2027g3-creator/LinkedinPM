## Complete LinkedIn Post Copy

Premature scaling in go-to-market is the number one cause of startup mortality.

Here is how the tragedy unfolds:
- A startup raises a $10M funding round.
- The board tells the founder: 'You have product-market fit. Now hire 12 account executives and scale!'
- The founder hires a VP of Sales, 10 reps, and 4 SDRs.
- Six months later, CAC has quadrupled, new reps have empty calendars, pipeline conversion is single-digit, and the company burns $600K a month with nothing to show for it.

What went wrong?
The company confused initial customer traction with a scalable commercial engine.

Before you hire a sales army, you must verify three GTM Scaling Milestones:

1. Repeatable Commercial ICP: Can you identify 500 accounts that share the exact same acute operational pain, budget profile, and technical infrastructure?
2. Repeatable Sales Playbook: Can a non-founder sales rep close deals at a consistent win rate (>25%) within a predictable timeframe without executive intervention?
3. Compounding Gross Margin: Do closed customers onboard smoothly and retain above 80% gross margin, or do they require bespoke engineering heroics?

If your sales process requires heroics to close, adding more reps will only accelerate your burn rate.

Engineer the machine first. Scale the capacity second.

Is your sales motion genuinely repeatable, or still dependent on founder heroics?
