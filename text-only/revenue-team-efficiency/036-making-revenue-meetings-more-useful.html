Production-Ready Copy

If your pipeline reviews consist of asking reps 'How are you feeling about this deal?', you are forecasting based on fiction.

Subjective optimism is the enemy of revenue predictability.

When pipeline inspections are unstructured:
- Charismatic reps tell compelling stories about deals that have been stalled for 4 months.
- Reserved reps under-forecast deals that are virtually guaranteed to close.
- Sales leaders build quarterly projections on narrative comfort rather than verifiable commercial milestones.

An effective, evidence-based pipeline inspection follows a strict 3-question diagnostic framework for every single qualified opportunity:

  1. What Verified Action Did the Buyer Take This Week? Did they attend a mutual action plan review? Did they introduce their security officer? Did they provide written feedback on the business case? (If no buyer action occurred, the deal did not advance).
  2. Who on the Buying Committee Has Not Yet Engaged? Have we validated access to the economic buyer who controls the budget, or are we single-threaded with an enthusiastic non-decision-maker?
  3. What Is the Explicit Consequence of Inaction Before the Close Date? If the contract is not signed by the target milestone, what measurable business penalty does the buyer incur?

When pipeline reviews inspect verified customer actions rather than rep feelings, forecast variance disappears and real deal coaching begins.
