## Complete LinkedIn Post Copy

If your sales enablement strategy relies on getting reps to watch 4-hour video courses on a Friday, your enablement program has already failed.

The dirty secret of corporate sales training is the 'Forgetting Curve':
- Sales reps forget 70% of what they learn in a training seminar within 24 hours.
- Within 30 days, 87% of the material is completely lost.
- Enablement teams spend months building certification modules that sellers click through on mute while checking email.

Enablement fails when it is delivered as an interruption to the selling day.

High-adoption enablement is delivered contextually, in the flow of work:

1. In-Call Guidance: Objection playbooks and value frameworks appear dynamically on the seller's screen during live customer conversations.
2. Automated Pre-Briefings: Enablement insights are embedded directly into the 360° pre-meeting dossier tailored to the specific prospect.
3. Continuous Micro-Coaching: Post-call intelligence flags missed qualification moments within minutes of hanging up, reinforcing behavior immediately.

Don't ask sellers to step away from their pipeline to learn. Put the learning directly into the tools they use to close deals.

How does your team ensure sales training actually transforms live customer conversations?
